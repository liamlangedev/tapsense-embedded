Set the real value
Change SHUNT_OHMS to the resistor actually installed.
Safety / correctness: Do not connect an unknown voltage or high-current load to GPIO34. Keep the ADC node between 0 V and 3.3 V, share GND, and use a proper current-sense amplifier for large current or a high-side measurement. A resistor alone cannot safely measure arbitrary current.
